How to Hook Viewers in the First 3 Seconds of a Reel

Audience retention is the single most critical ranking factor for short-form video algorithms. A compelling hook prevents viewers from swiping away.

1. Open with Curiosity or Contradiction

Start with statements like: "Stop doing this mistake...", "Why 90% fail at...", or "The secret framework nobody talks about...".

2. Combine Spoken Words with Bold On-Screen Text

Over 50% of users scroll through Instagram with sound turned off. Clear, animated text overlay ensures your message connects immediately.

3. Include Immediate Visual Movement

Show physical action, a fast transition, or an intriguing gesture in frame right as the video starts to hold visual focus.

4. State the Value Upfront

Tell the audience exactly what solution or knowledge they will get by staying until the very end of your Reel.
