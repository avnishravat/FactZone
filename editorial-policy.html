Editorial Policy

How FactZone creates, researches, reviews, updates, and corrects its content.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. About Our Editorial Policy

FactZone is an independently operated informational website that publishes articles covering interesting facts, science, history, technology, everyday phenomena, and other informational topics.

This Editorial Policy explains how we approach content creation, research, factual accuracy, corrections, updates, and the use of technology such as artificial intelligence.

Our goal is to provide readers with useful, understandable, and responsibly presented information.

2. How We Create Content

Articles published on FactZone may be researched and prepared using publicly available information, reliable reference materials, research publications, institutional sources, and other relevant sources.

Content is written and edited to make complex or interesting subjects easier for readers to understand while avoiding intentionally misleading claims.

Headlines and introductions may use curiosity-driven language, but they should remain consistent with the actual subject discussed in the article.

3. Sources & Research

The sources used for an article depend on the subject and the type of claim being discussed.

Where appropriate, research may include:

  • Scientific research papers and academic publications
  • Universities and research institutions
  • Government and public institutions
  • Official organizations and institutional websites
  • Historical archives, museums, and established reference sources
  • Original studies and primary sources
  • Reputable reporting for developing or widely reported events

When a claim is important, surprising, numerical, scientific, medical, historical, or attributed to a specific organization, additional verification may be appropriate before publication.

4. Fact-Checking & Accuracy

FactZone aims to review factual claims before publication, particularly claims that could materially affect a reader's understanding of a subject.

During review, individual claims may be compared with available reliable sources rather than treating an entire article as automatically accurate.

Examples of claims that may require additional verification include:

  • Statistics and numerical figures
  • Scientific findings
  • Medical or health-related claims
  • Historical dates and events
  • Names, organizations, and attributed statements
  • Claims referring to a specific study
  • Claims attributed to government agencies or institutions
  • Unusual or viral claims that require stronger evidence

When evidence does not fully support a strong statement, wording may be changed to reflect the available evidence more accurately.

FactZone does not assign an artificial accuracy percentage to an article. Accuracy is considered at the level of individual factual claims and the evidence available for them.

5. Use of Artificial Intelligence

Technology, including artificial intelligence tools, may be used during parts of the content creation process.

AI-assisted content is not treated as automatically factual. Information generated by an AI system may contain errors, outdated information, unsupported claims, or incorrect interpretations.

Therefore, relevant factual information may require additional research, verification, editing, and human review before publication.

AI tools may assist with tasks such as drafting, organizing, brainstorming, summarizing, or improving readability, while editorial responsibility remains with the publisher.

6. Corrections & Updates

If a factual error is identified after publication, FactZone may update the article to correct or clarify the information.

Updates may also be made when new reliable information becomes available or when an existing explanation requires clarification.

Where appropriate, significant updates may be reflected through an updated date on the article.

Readers who notice a possible factual error are encouraged to contact FactZone with the relevant article and information supporting the correction.

7. Originality & Copyright

FactZone aims to publish original editorial content and does not intentionally reproduce copyrighted material without an appropriate legal basis or permission.

Information, facts, ideas, and publicly available knowledge may be discussed in original wording. When external material is referenced, appropriate attribution or linking may be provided where applicable.

Images and other media used on the website are intended to be used according to their applicable licenses, permissions, or usage rights.

8. Headlines & Presentation

FactZone may use engaging headlines designed to encourage readers to explore an article.

However, headlines should not intentionally present a claim that the article itself does not support.

We aim to distinguish between established information, emerging findings, interpretations, and claims that remain uncertain.

9. Editorial Responsibility

FactZone is independently operated. Editorial responsibility includes deciding what content to publish, reviewing available information, correcting identified errors, and updating published material when appropriate.

The availability of an article on FactZone does not mean that every statement should be interpreted as universally established fact. Some subjects may involve uncertainty, ongoing research, differing interpretations, or limited available evidence.

Readers should consider the context and sources provided when evaluating information, particularly for medical, financial, legal, or other high-impact decisions.

10. Reader Feedback

Reader feedback can help identify unclear explanations, outdated information, or potential factual errors.

If you believe an article contains an incorrect or misleading factual statement, please contact us with:

  • The article URL
  • The specific statement you believe is incorrect
  • A reliable source or evidence supporting the correction
  • Any additional context that may help us review the issue

11. Transparency

FactZone aims to be transparent about how its content is produced and maintained.

This policy may be updated as our editorial processes evolve. The latest version will be available on this page.
