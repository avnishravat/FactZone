Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is it free to download YouTube thumbnails on FactZone?

Can I download thumbnails from YouTube Shorts?

Yes! Simply copy the URL of any YouTube Short (e.g. youtube.com/shorts/...) and paste it into our tool.

Why is Maximum Resolution (1080p) not showing for some videos?

If the original creator uploaded a low-resolution video or did not supply a high-resolution custom thumbnail, YouTube fallback defaults to HD (720p) or HQ (480p).
