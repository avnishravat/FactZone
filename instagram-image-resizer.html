Official Instagram Image Sizes (2026 Standards)

To avoid blurry compression or awkward automated cropping, always convert photos to match Instagram's standard dimensions:

  • Square Post (1:1): 1080 x 1080 px
  • Portrait Post (4:5): 1080 x 1350 px (Recommended for feed coverage)
  • Instagram Story & Reel (9:16): 1080 x 1920 px
  • Landscape Post (1.91:1): 1080 x 566 px

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best quality format for Instagram?

JPG saved between 85% to 90% quality works best for standard photos. Use PNG for illustrations or graphics with text.

Are my photos uploaded to any server?

No, 100% private. All rendering happens locally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas technology.
