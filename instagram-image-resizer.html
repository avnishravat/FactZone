Official Instagram Image Size Guide (2026 Standards)

To keep your feed looking sharp and prevent Instagram from applying blurry compression, always upload images that match the platform's native aspect ratios:

  • Square Post (1:1): 1080 x 1080 px
  • Portrait Post (4:5): 1080 x 1350 px (Recommended for maximum vertical feed real estate)
  • Instagram Story & Reel (9:16): 1080 x 1920 px
  • Landscape Post (1.91:1): 1080 x 566 px

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best format to post on Instagram?

JPG format saved at 85%–90% quality is ideal for photos. PNG is best if your image contains text or transparent graphics.

Does this image resizer lower photo quality?

No. We use browser-native HTML5 Canvas algorithms with high-quality smoothing to ensure your photos remain crisp.

Is my data private?

Yes, 100% private. Your image files never leave your device or get uploaded to any servers.
