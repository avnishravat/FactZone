How to Optimize Images Online

FactZone's Image & Design Tools process your images locally in your web browser for fast optimization and maximum privacy. Follow these steps:

  1. Select Image: Click the file input box to upload your image file (PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG).
  2. Optimize: Click on 'Optimize Image' to trigger client-side canvas rendering.
  3. Preview & Download: View your processed image instant preview and click 'Download Image' to save.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does client-side image processing work?

Client-side image processing runs directly inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript API. Your image never uploads to an external server.

What image formats are supported?

Our tool supports all popular image formats including PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, SVG, and GIF.

Are my images private and secure?

Yes, 100%. Since processing happens locally on your device, your photos never leave your web browser.
